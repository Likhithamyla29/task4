# Coffee Sales Chain Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee sales performance using Power BI. The dashboard provides interactive insights into sales, profit, expenses, product performance, and market trends. It helps stakeholders monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.

## Dataset

- Dataset Name: Coffee Sales Chain Analysis
- Tool Used: Power BI
- Dataset Type: Sales and Financial Analysis

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Identify top-performing states and product lines.
- Compare actual sales against target sales.
- Evaluate market size contribution to total sales.
- Provide interactive filtering for detailed analysis.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Expenses
- Profit Margin %

### Interactive Slicers
- Date
- State
- Market
- Product Line

### Visualizations
- Sales Trend by Year (Line Chart)
- Sales by State (Bar Chart)
- Sales by Product Line (Bar Chart)
- Profit by Product Type (Bar Chart)
- Market Size Contribution (Donut Chart)
- Actual Sales vs Target Sales (Clustered Column Chart)

## Key Insights

- Total Sales reached 203K.
- Total Profit generated was 64K.
- Profit Margin achieved 31.7%.
- California recorded the highest sales performance.
- Beans generated higher sales compared to Leaves.
- Espresso was among the most profitable product types.
- Actual sales closely matched target sales across major product lines.

## Conclusion

The Coffee Sales Chain Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance through interactive visualizations and KPIs. It enables stakeholders to identify trends, monitor profitability, evaluate product performance, and make informed business decisions.
